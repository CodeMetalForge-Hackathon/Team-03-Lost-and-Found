Positions come from range alone (sonar dead reckoning): one forward ultrasonic ranger at 1 Hz, with no heading, odometry, GPS or camera. A jump in range means the dog turned, but the angle of the turn is unknown. TILT alerts while MOVING are gait bob. BARK means a loud sound, not a confirmed bark. Losing the echo means open floor ahead, not a fault. The beam is credited 0.9 m with no echo (where its lower edge meets the floor). The thermistor is a contact sensor, not IR.
