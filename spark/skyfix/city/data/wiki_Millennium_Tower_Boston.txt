'''Millennium Tower''' is a 60-story, {{convert|684|ft|m|adj=mid|-tall}} residential skyscraper in [[Boston]], Massachusetts, United States.<ref name="Luxury Boston">{{cite web|title=Millennium Tower Boston – Pre-Construction|url=https://www.luxuryboston.com/Millennium-Tower|website=Luxury Boston|accessdate=September 27, 2016}}</ref> Construction began in 2013 at the site of the former flagship store for [[Filene's]] in [[Downtown Crossing]] and was completed in 2016.<ref name="construction"/> {{as of|2022}}, it is the fifth [[List of tallest buildings in Boston|tallest building in Boston]]. The building contains 442 [[condominium]]s, a [[Roche Bros.]] grocery store and [[Office#Grading|Class A office space]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://handelarch.com/projects/type/office/millennium-tower-boston-office.html|title=Millennium Tower Boston|work=handelarch.com|accessdate=May 5, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170609200431/http://handelarch.com/projects/type/office/millennium-tower-boston-office.html|archive-date=June 9, 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.cosentini.com/index.php/portfolio-articles/34-residential/142-millennium-tower |title=Millennium Tower |website=Cosentini Associates |access-date=February 6, 2020}}</ref> Limited occupancy by residents began in July 2016.<ref name="construction"/>

==History==

===Initial plans, demolition, and project halt===
Millennium Tower and the Burnham Building occupy the space of the former flagship [[Filene's Department Store|Filene's store]]. When Filene's closed, the building was put up for sale and was bought by [[Vornado Realty Trust]] of New York. Vornado teamed up with Gale International for a $700 million redevelopment of the Filene's site.

As approved in August 2007,<ref name=BGlobeAug2007>{{cite news| url=http://archive.boston.com/business/globe/articles/2007/08/18/city_oks_redevelopment_of_historic_filenes_block/ |work=The Boston Globe |title=City OK's redevelopment of historic Filene's block |date=August 18, 2007 | url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070820141516/https://www.boston.com/business/globe/articles/2007/08/18/city_oks_redevelopment_of_historic_filenes_block/ |archive-date=August 20, 2007}}</ref> the site redevelopment project consisted of a 39-story tower including a 280-room hotel, a 125-seat restaurant, {{convert|475000|sqft|m2}} of office space, 166 residential condos, {{convert|300000|sqft|m2}} of retail space, and an adjacent park. It was originally scheduled for completion by 2011.<ref name=BGlobeAug2007/> In order to gut the historic Filene's building and demolish an adjacent building, the project required closing [[Filene's Basement]] and the loss of 218 retail jobs.<ref name=BGlobeNov2010>{{cite news |last1=Ross |first1=Casey |title=Filene's site permits revoked |url=https://archive.boston.com/business/articles/2010/11/18/filenes_site_permits_revoked/ |accessdate=March 18, 2020 |work=Boston.com |date=November 18, 2010}}</ref>

In Summer of 2008, the developers halted the project, citing the [[2008 financial crisis]]. As of the halt, demolition was nearly complete, leaving the Burnham building gutted and a large excavation where the non-landmarked parts of the former Filenes complex (built in the 1950s–1970s) had stood. The side of the Burnham building that was exposed by the nearby demolition was temporarily covered with a tarp.  After two years of inactivity, the City of Boston revoked the developer's permits in November 2010.<ref name=BGlobeNov2010/>

===Ownership change and construction start===
In 2012, Millennium Partners took over the task and won approval for a 625-foot tower with 1.2 million square feet (110,000 m<sup>2</sup>) of commercial and residential space in two buildings.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Grillo |first1=Thomas |title=City approves 625-foot tower at former Filene's site |url=https://www.bizjournals.com/boston/real_estate/2012/09/bra-approves-millennium-filenes-tower.html |accessdate=March 18, 2020 |work=www.bizjournals.com}}</ref>

On April 13, 2013, major tenant [[Arnold Worldwide]] was signed to occupy a large part of the new building. With the agreement in place, construction resumed.<ref name="globe-2013-04-12">{{cite web|title=Filene's project to resume as major tenant signed|url=http://www.boston.com/businessupdates/2013/04/12/filene-project-resume-major-tenant-signed/BO3q3EP2cBIfC6Mf0WxNyH/story.html|work=Boston Globe|accessdate=September 12, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130414231904/http://www.boston.com/businessupdates/2013/04/12/filene-project-resume-major-tenant-signed/BO3q3EP2cBIfC6Mf0WxNyH/story.html |archive-date=April 14, 2013}}</ref> The building officially broke ground on September 17, 2013 with mayor [[Thomas M. Menino]] in attendance.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.metro.us/newyork/news/2013/09/17/millenium-tower-boston-breaks-ground-on-new-downtown-crossing-skyscraper/|title=Millennium Tower: Boston breaks ground on new Downtown Crossing skyscraper|work=www.metro.us|date=September 17, 2013|accessdate=March 25, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608003807/http://www.metro.us/local/millennium-tower-boston-breaks-ground-on-new-skyscraper/tmWmiq---fecvpUGLTpKg2/|archive-date=June 8, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> On April 26, 2014, 600 trucks poured {{convert|6,000|cuyd|m3}} of concrete into a slab for the new building, making it the largest pour in the city's history.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://boston.cbslocal.com/2014/04/26/record-concrete-pour-paves-way-for-millennium-tower-at-downtown-crossing/|title=Record Concrete Pour Paves Way For Millennium Tower At Downtown Crossing|publisher=CBS Boston|date=April 26, 2014|accessdate=May 26, 2014}}</ref>

===Recent developments and tenants===
Tenants, as of 2021, include [[Roche Bros]] supermarket.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://dailyfreepress.com/2013/09/24/millennium-tower-to-add-to-boston-skyline-as-4th-tallest-building/|title=Millennium Tower to add to Boston skyline as 4th-tallest building|work=dailyfreepress.com|date=September 24, 2013|accessdate=March 25, 2014}}</ref>

According to an August 2016 article in the ''[[Boston Globe]]'', luxury condos have been purchased by local residents as well buyers from [[Asia]] and [[Europe]]. A Millennium executive estimated at the time that 75% of the owners will be living in their units in Downtown Crossing.<ref>{{cite news |last=Tim |first=Logan |url=https://www.bostonglobe.com/business/2016/08/29/with-millennium-tower-boston-reaches-new-heights-foreign-money-magnet/keC5sjXE546tpvGmGsTVlM/story.html |title=Foreign buyers at Millennium Tower show that Boston is on a world stage |work=[[The Boston Globe]] |date=August 30, 2016 |accessdate=August 30, 2016 }}</ref>
